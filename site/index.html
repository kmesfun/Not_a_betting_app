What happens when news breaks

The gap has a shelf life.

Prediction markets absorb news well — eventually. The window between the report landing and the price moving is the only thing this product sells. Drag across the chart to move through the night.

19:58 · The report

News lands

A wire report arrives as prose. We read it into a structured roster event — who, how long, how much it matters — and hold anything the model isn't sure about for a human.

20:41 · The reprice

Our model moves

The team's rating drops, the season is re-simulated 50,000 times, and every downstream probability moves with it. Championship, conference, playoff odds — all at once.

Next morning · The gap

The market lags

Contracts are still priced off last night's board. We measure the difference in standard errors, not raw points, so a wide gap on a noisy estimate doesn't get sent as a signal.

Why 50,000 and not 1,000

Every probability is an estimate. Here's how wrong.

Each dot is one complete run of the simulator on the same season. If we only ran 1,000 seasons, a genuine 5% longshot would come back anywhere from about 3.7% to 6.4% — enough to invent a gap that isn't there.

±1 standard error — Relative error — 40 runs landed between —
Dots are 40 fixed draws from the binomial sampling distribution, rescaled as the sample size changes — the spread you see is the real sampling error.

What this isn't

Not a sportsbook. Not a tout.

This is an analytics product with an independence thesis. The moment it takes a cut of your action, the numbers stop being worth reading — so it doesn't.

  • ✕No bets placed, no money held, no links into a wagering flow
  • ✕No affiliate revenue from any book or exchange
  • ✕No profit or ROI claims, ever
  • ✕No cherry-picked record — losing stretches are published too

Where the numbers come from

Reproducible, or it doesn't ship.

Every probability is a Monte Carlo output you could regenerate from the same inputs. A language model reads the news into labels; the arithmetic stays in code, where it can be audited and fitted.

  • →50,000 simulations per run, with a standard error on every figure
  • →Market prices devigged across the complete field before comparison
  • →Alerts gated on model uncertainty, not just the size of the gap
  • →Quiet days send nothing at all
